2. Data kept on your device

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

To work, the app stores the following in a local database (SQLite) and app settings on your device only. We do not receive a copy:

  • your test attempts, answers, scores, time per question and flagged questions;
  • your per-question learning statistics (used to choose which questions to show you again) and bookmarks;
  • your study plan: test date (for the countdown), daily goal and reminder time;
  • your daily progress, used for streaks;
  • app preferences such as light or dark mode, and whether you own Premium (so the app works offline);
  • the downloaded question bank and study notes;
  • a copy of any question report you make (see section 7.3).

Read aloud uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. The text is spoken on your device and we do not send it anywhere, although your device maker's speech service may follow its own settings.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it in a backup under your account with that provider.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app is funded by ads from Google AdMob: banner ads (including a collapsible banner), native ads shown among the content, full-screen ads shown between screens (for example after a mock exam), and optional reward videos that you can choose to watch to unlock an extra.

Consent. When you open the app, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent message where the law requires one, including in the UK and the European Economic Area. Ads are only requested after this step. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

iPhone and iPad. After the consent message, iOS asks whether the app may track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the app cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What Google may collect through the AdMob SDK includes: your device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID or iOS IDFA, where allowed), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, app information, and how you interact with ads (views and taps). Google uses this to serve, measure and cap the frequency of ads, prevent fraud and, if you consent, personalise ads. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use data only for purposes such as delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google acts as an independent controller for much of this processing. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Premium and ads. Premium removes video ads and full-screen ads. Banner and native ads are still shown to Premium users.

4. Premium purchases (RevenueCat)

Premium is a one-off purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; Apple or Google handles payment under their own terms and privacy policies.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Premium and to restore purchases on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID that it creates, your purchase receipt and transaction details (product, price, currency, date and store country), and basic device and app information such as operating system, app version and IP address. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf as a processor. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Content updates

To fix and improve questions and study notes without an app update, the app checks for new content at most about once a day. It downloads files over an encrypted HTTPS connection from a server we control and checks each file against a security hash before using it. The request contains no personal information beyond what any internet request carries (your IP address and basic technical details such as the time of the request), which the hosting provider may record in its server logs.

6. Daily reminders

If you turn on daily study reminders, the reminder is scheduled as a local notification on your device. No data is sent to us or to a push notification service. Your device asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off in the app or in your device's notification settings.

7. Planned features and question reports

Sections 7.1 and 7.2 are not live yet (section 7.3 is live). We are describing them now so the policy does not need to change suddenly when they arrive. Until a feature is released in an app update, the app does not send the data described in this section. We will update the effective date of this policy when they go live.

7.1 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Planned

If the app crashes, it would send a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the bug. A report contains the error and the code where it happened, device model, operating system and app version, free memory and disk space, whether the app was in the foreground, and a random installation identifier. It does not include your answers or anything you type. This will only be sent if you consent.

7.2 Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) Planned

With your consent, the app would send a small set of usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which features help people prepare. The events we plan to record are: finishing the welcome screens, starting and finishing a test (with the test type, score and whether it was a pass), reporting a question, viewing the Premium screen, buying Premium and meeting your daily goal. Firebase also records an app instance ID, device and app information and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. We will not send your name, email or any free text to Analytics. Consent is asked through the same consent message as for ads, and you can change it in Settings → Privacy options.

7.3 Question reports and feedback (email)

This feature is live today and works by email. If you report a question or send feedback, the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to vkhorizon.lab@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you do, we receive your email address, your message (the question's ID and text, the reason you picked and any note) and anything you add. We use it only to fix the content and to reply to you. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). A copy of each question report is also kept on your device. In future, reports may instead be sent anonymously, without your email address, to our database in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) Planned.

For Firebase, Google acts as our processor. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

11. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention policy. See How Google retains data.
  • Purchase records (RevenueCat): for as long as needed to provide and restore Premium, and for up to 6 years after the end of the tax year of purchase, for tax and accounting.
  • Content server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Crash reports Planned: up to 90 days.
  • Analytics events Planned: up to 14 months.
  • Question reports and feedback emails: up to 24 months, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights and choices

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, for data you gave us under consent or a contract;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew it.

Useful controls:

  • Settings → Privacy options in the app: change your ad consent (shown in the UK and EEA).
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: allow or stop tracking.
  • Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads: delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete everything stored on your device.

Because there are no accounts, we usually cannot link data to you by name. To help us find data about you (for example a purchase), we may ask for details such as your store order number. We will reply within one month.
